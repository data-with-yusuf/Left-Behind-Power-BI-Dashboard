# Limitations

- Data coverage varies by country, indicator, sex, and year; comparisons may have different country coverage.
- Some source observations may be unavailable for 2023. The report does not silently substitute the latest year; missing selected-year observations remain blank.
- Gross enrollment ratios can exceed 100%.
- Education expenditure as a percentage of GDP is a broad spending context measure, not per-student expenditure or a direct efficiency measure.
- Spending and governance comparisons are exploratory and do not establish causation.
- Completion Gender Gap is described in project notes as male completion minus female completion; verify the DAX formula before publication.
- A progress visual was excluded from V1 because anomalous time-series patterns were observed, including identical values across years for Moldova.
- Previously noted questions about global out-of-school totals and the 2023 gender-parity blank should be rechecked or clearly disclosed.
